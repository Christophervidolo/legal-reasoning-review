## Methodology and limitations

The evaluator uses deterministic pattern checks and a five-part rubric covering citation quality, issue coverage, jurisdiction fit, reasoning and uncertainty, and consumer safety.

Scores are preliminary screening results, not a determination of legal correctness. Citation presence does not prove an authority is current, binding, or applicable. Keyword matching does not prove complete issue coverage. The authority links and statutory text require independent verification by a qualified reviewer.

This report describes a constructed pilot dataset, not a dataset provided by the task platform. It is not legal advice.
